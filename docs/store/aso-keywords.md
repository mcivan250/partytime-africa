## Google Play

**Title (≤30):** `Party Time: Kampala Events`
Alternates to A/B test: `Party Time – Events & Tickets` · `Party Time: Kampala Nightlife`

**Short description (≤80):**
`Discover events in Kampala tonight, buy tickets safely & promote your event.`

- Opening (first ~150 chars, shown before "read more"): "Party Time is Kampala's home for nightlife — find events happening in Uganda tonight, buy tickets safely, and promote your own event."
- Section "What's on in Kampala tonight" — events, concerts, parties, raves, festivals, rooftop nights, live DJ sets, afrobeats, amapiano.
- Section "Buy event tickets safely in Uganda" — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, instant digital tickets, QR entry, the **Buyer Guarantee** (name it — competitors have no trust language).
- Section "Discover the best bars, rooftops & clubs in Kampala" — venue discovery, bar/pub/nightclub listings, near me, opening hours.
- Section "Promote your event in Kampala" — list events, sell tickets, reach the nightlife crowd, track sales.
- Section "Why Party Time" — social proof ("🔥 N going"), free to download, made in Kampala.
- Keywords to weave in 2–3x each: Kampala events, Uganda events, events tonight, nightlife, buy tickets, event tickets Uganda, concerts Kampala, parties, bars, rooftops, clubs, promote event, sell tickets, mobile money.
- CTA close: "Download Party Time and never miss a night out in Kampala."
